English

In short: EGOSS Bot is the private support bot of the EgossPACK Discord server. Use it to get help, do not abuse it, and follow Discord's rules.

1. Acceptance

By using EGOSS Bot ("the bot") in the EgossPACK Discord server, you agree to these terms, to our Privacy Policy and to Discord's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

2. What the bot does

The bot provides a support panel, answers to frequently asked questions, channel search, pack recommendations and private support tickets. It only works inside the EgossPACK server.

3. Acceptable use

  • Open tickets only for real support requests. The bot allows up to 3 tickets per 24 hours and one open ticket at a time.
  • Do not spam, harass staff, share harmful or illegal content, or try to break, overload or misuse the bot.
  • Do not share personal data of other people in tickets.

4. Tickets

  • Staff may close a ticket when the issue is solved, when it breaks these terms, or after a period of inactivity. Inactive tickets are closed automatically.
  • If you leave the server, your open ticket is closed automatically.
  • Closed ticket conversations are recorded as described in the Privacy Policy.

5. Enforcement

If you break these terms, staff may close your tickets and restrict your access to the bot or the server.

6. No warranty

The bot and its answers are provided "as is". We try to keep it available and accurate, but we do not guarantee that it will always work or that every answer fits your system. We are not liable for any damage resulting from use of the bot or its recommendations, to the extent permitted by law.

7. Changes

We may change the bot or these terms at any time. Changes are published on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

8. Contact

Questions about these terms: open a ticket in the EgossPACK server or contact the server owner on Discord.

EGOSS Bot'u ("bot") EgossPACK Discord sunucusunda kullanarak bu koşulları, Gizlilik Politikamızı, Discord Hizmet Koşulları'nı ve Topluluk Kuralları'nı kabul etmiş olursun.

Bot; destek paneli, sık sorulan soru cevapları, kanal arama, pack önerisi ve özel destek ticket'ları sağlar. Sadece EgossPACK sunucusunda çalışır.

3. Kullanım kuralları
